# Lillia Dream MTG Deck

Concept: Cards in hand are dreams, and Lillia gets stronger when opponents’ dreams are stronger.

Win condition: Make opponents draw lots of cards, then one shot them with massive damage that scales with the number of cards they have in hand.

## Sleeping emblem:

“You can’t play cards from your hand. The next time you take damage, take additional damage equal to your hand size and destroy this emblem. Whenever you discard a card, if you have hand size of 7 or less, destroy this emblem.”

# Lillia, Bashful Bloom

4 cost, 0/1  
Legendary Enchantment Elk Centaur Creature  
lillia gains +X/+X and Ward X, where X is the biggest hand size among your opponents.  
tap: target opponent draws 1 card  
opponents have no hand size limit

# Watch out! Eep!

sorcery, 2 cost  
Target opponent draws 3 cards. Deal 1 damage to that opponent.

# Blooming Blows

sorcery, 2 cost  
Each opponent draws 1 card and takes 1 damage.

# Lilting Lullaby

sorcery 3 cost  
all opponents with hand size 7 or more gets a Sleeping emblem (“You can’t play cards from your hand. The next time you take damage, take additional damage equal to your hand size and destroy this emblem. Whenever you discard a card, if you have hand size of 7 or less, destroy this emblem.”)

# Fawn Mower

1 cost instant  
Target opponent draws 1 card. deal 1 damage to up to X creatures that opponent controls, where X is their hand size.

# Shared Dream

2 cost enchantment  
At the beginning of each draw step, each player draws 1 card. Sleeping players draw 2 instead.

# Bashful Bloom

2 cost enchantment  
at the start of your turn, if an opponents hand size is greater than your life total, gain 1 life.  
Whenever a source a player controls deals damage to you, that player discards 1 card.

# Dreaming Tree

1GG, 0/3  
legendary enchantment creature – plant

Defender, Reach

Dreaming Tree gets +0/+X, where X is the current player’s handsize.

Whenever a creature becomes blocked by Dreaming Tree, that creature’s controller gets a Sleeping emblem.

At the beginning of each player’s end step, if they didn’t play any spells during their turn, they draw 1 card.

# Dream Journal

2 cost artifact  
Whenever an opponent draws a card outside their draw step, you may return an instant or sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand.

# Lillia, Dream Realizer

3 cost, 0/1  
legendary enchantment elk centaur creature

Ward – Sacrifice a Land.

Lands opponents control have “Return this land to its owner's hand: Gain 1 mana of any color.”

Whenever a permanent enters the battlefield under an opponent's control, you gain 1 life.

*"Every dream I helped restore, made me stronger than before." -Lillia*

# Dream-Laden Bough

3 cost equipment  
Equip creature 1  
1 mana, Tap: Choose target player. Deal 1 damage to X creatures that player controls, where X is that player’s hand size.

# Swirlseed

1 cost instant  
Target opponent Scrys X, where X is their hand size. Then they draw 1 card. If it is a creature card, they draw 2 more cards.

# Bonk!

2 cost instant  
Deal X damage to target creature, where X is that creature’s controller’s hand size. If that creature is tapped, increase the damage by your hand size. Excess damage is dealt to that creature’s controller.

# Lillia, Strong Capable Dream Fawn

5 cost, 5/5  
legendary enchantment elk centaur creature  
Trample  
Whenever an opponent draws a card outside their draw step, Lillia does 1 damage to them.  
Other elk centaur creatures you control have +1/+0 and trample.

# Root Infection

1 cost instant  
Destroy target artifact or enchantment with converted mana cost X or less, where X is that artifact’s or enchantment’s controller’s hand size.

# Thought Cloud

3 mana enchantment  
Enchant target opponent  
Enchanted opponent plays with their hand revealed. At the start of their draw step, they may Scry X, where X is their hand size. At the end of their turn, if their hand size is 7 or more, they get a Sleeping emblem.

# Dream Melon Garden

2 mana enchantment  
2 mana: Draw a card. At the end of your turn, if your hand size is 7 or more, you get a Sleeping emblem. Any player may activate this ability.

# Prance

1 mana instant  
Tap target permanent you control. If you do, it gains Hexproof until the start of your next turn.

# Leaf Grazer

2 cost, 2/2  
elk creature  
tap, 1 mana: draw a card

# Brush Clearer

1 cost, 1/1  
elk creature  
tap: add 1 mana. If an opponent has a hand size of 7 or more, add 2 mana instead

# Bark Scraper

3 cost, 1/3  
elk creature  
tap: tap target land an opponent controls

# REM Sleep

1 cost instant  
target opponent with hand size 4 or more draws 3 cards. Draw 2 cards.

# Thought Purge

1 cost instant

Choose a card from target player’s hand and reveal it. They take damage equal to its converted mana cost.
