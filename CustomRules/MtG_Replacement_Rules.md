MtG Replacement

Trying to make a game that’s like MtG but without the parts that I don’t like.

# Goal

Be the last player standing.

# Setup

Each player does these steps:

1.  Make a deck with at least 60 cards.
    1.  No more than 4 copies of any one card.
    2.  Must have at least 1 creature.
2.  Choose a creature card from your deck to be your character.
    1.  Place your character in your battle zone.
    2.  If its defense is less than 20, give it shield counters until its defense is 20.
3.  Shuffle your library.
4.  Draw 7 cards.
5.  Create your battle zone, mana pool, and energy pool.

# Gameplay

Each player takes their turn one at a time in order.

## Turn Phases

Each turn consists of these phases:

1.  Morning phase
    1.  Gain mana
    2.  Gain energy
    3.  Untap permanents
    4.  Draw a card
2.  Day phase
    1.  Play cards (optional)
    2.  Activate abilities (optional)
    3.  Attack (optional)
3.  Evening phase
    1.  Discard down to your hand size limit (7)

## Morning Phase

At the start of your turn, you do these things.

Gain mana. You always gain at least 1 mana. For each land you control, collect its passive mana. Add the mana to your mana pool. You have no max mana and mana carries over to the next round.

Gain energy. You don’t automatically gain energy. For each creature you control, you gain at least 1 energy. Add the energy to your energy pool.

Untap all permanents you control: creatures, artifacts, and lands. Untap your character.

Draw a card from your library. If your library is empty, your character takes 1 damage.

## Day Phase

On your turn, during your Day phase, you can:

-   Play cards
-   Activate abilities
-   Attack

### Play cards

Each card has a mana cost to play. To cast it, remove the required mana from your mana pool.

When you play a land, you can spend any amount of extra mana. The land enters play in your battle zone with your claim tokens on it. The amount of claim tokens is equal to the amount of mana you spent to play it.

### Activate abilities

Some permanents have activatable abilities that cost energy to activate. To activate it, remove the required energy from your energy pool.

### Attack

Each untapped creature can attack on your turn. To have a creature attack, tap it and declare which target it is attacking.

If the target is a creature, both creatures deal damage to each other at the same time. The amount of damage a creature deals is equal to their attack stat. Their attack stat is equal to their bonus attack plus their power (default bonus attack is 0). Damage is dealt to creatures in wound counters. When has an amount of wound counters equal to or greater than its defense, it dies and is sent to the graveyard.

If the target is an artifact, the attacking creature deals damage to the artifact. If the amount of damage is equal to or greater than the artifact’s defense, the artifact is destroyed and is sent to the graveyard.

If the target is a land, the attacking creature adds your claim tokens and/or removes claim tokens of another player. The amount of claim tokens it adds and/or removes is equal to its power. If you have the most claim tokens on a land, you control it.

If the target is multiple types, all applicable rules apply to it.

## On other players’ turns

There are things you can do when it’s not your turn. When another player plays a card, activates an ability, or attacks, you can react to it.

You can react by playing a card, activating an ability, or blocking.

### Play a card

You can play a card if that card has the Instant keyword. Its effect resolves before the effect of the action before it.

### Activate an ability

You can activate the ability of a creature you control. Spend the required amount of energy from your energy pool to activate it.

However, if the previous action was a creature ability activation, your creature must have power equal to or less than the other creature’s power in order to interrupt it *(“Smaller” creatures are faster)*.

### Block

You can block an attack. When an attack is declared, you can tap a creature you control to block the attack, causing the attack to instead target the blocking creature.

You can block a spell cast. When a spell targets one or more permanents, you can tap a creature you control to block the spell, causing it to change one of its targets to the blocking creature.

You can block an activated ability. When an activated ability targets one or more permanents, you can tap a creature you control to block the ability, causing it to change one of its targets to the blocking creature.

When you block, if the blocking creature is not a valid target for the spell or activated ability, then the spell or ability is wasted.

# Changes

-   Mana is always colorless.
-   You gain mana passively each turn.
-   There’s no max mana.
-   Mana carries over between turns.
-   Mana is used to cast spells and summon creatures.
-   Energy is used to activate creature abilities.
-   You passively gain energy each turn.
-   The card types are Creature, Land, Cast, Artifact.
-   You can tap a creature to attack.
-   You can attack creatures, artifacts, and lands.
-   You can tap a creature to block, changing the target of the attack, spell, or ability to the blocking creature.
-   Creatures don’t auto-heal at the end of each turn.
-   You no longer have a health pool.
-   You choose a creature to be your main character card.
-   Each creature has a power stat, which is their combined attack and defense.
-   Each creature may have a sword stat for additional attack, or a shield stat for additional defense.
-   Each creature has a mana cost to cast.
-   Each creature can have special abilities that trigger under certain conditions or cost energy to activate.
-   There is no max hand size. You still start with 7 cards, and draw 1 card from your library each turn.
-   When you go to draw a card while your library is empty, your character loses 1 health.
-   When your character drops to 0 health or less, you lose.
-   Each player has a hand, library, graveyard, exile pile, mana pool, energy pool, and battle zone.
-   Cast cards are like the old sorceries.
-   Cast cards with the Instant keyword can be cast in reaction to other cards.
-   Trample, Flying, Reach, Deathtouch, First Strike, Double Strike, Menace, Vigilance are all still a thing.
    -   Trample means excess damage is dealt to the original target.
    -   Lifelink is replaced by Lifesteal, which heals the combating creature.
    -   Vigilance means that the creature can untap after attacking or blocking.
-   Lands passively and/or actively give you mana or energy.
-   “Enchantment” is a keyword for Cast cards that makes the effect permanent.
-   Artifacts cant attack or block, unless it is also a Creature.
-   Lands can be conquered. Instead of dealing damage, a creature can add and remove claim tokens. The player who has the most claim tokens on a land controls it.
-   Flash is now called “Instant”.
-   Split-second is not a keyword.
-   The library min size will still be 60.

# Card Types

Card types: Creature, Cast, Artifact, Land, Character.

Creatures get summoned using mana. They stay on the battlefield and attack and defend, and have abilities. Creatures that are summoned have summoning sickness until your next end step. Creatures with summoning sickness cant attack and cant activate their activable abilities. Creatures with summoning sickness can still block and can still trigger their triggered abilities and have their passive abilities active. Creatures have vitality (health) and power (attack). Creatures can target other permanents to attack, and creatures can be targeted by attacks. Creatures deal damage equal to their power. When a creature takes damage, it gets a hit counter. If a creature has hit counters equal to or greater than its vitality, it dies and goes to the graveyard. If another permanent you control is being targeted for an attack, you may block that attack with a creature. The blocking creature takes the damage instead, and deals damage to the attacking creature or character. If another permanent you control is being targeted by a spell or ability, you may block that spell or ability with a creature, and have that spell or ability target that creature instead. If a creature is not a valid target for the spell or ability it blocks, the spell or ability does nothing. A creature can block only once per turn.

Invocation (Cast) cards get played from the hand using mana, take their effect, and then return to the bottom of the library.

Artifacts get crafted using mana. They stay on the battlefield and have abilities. Artifacts do not have summoning sickness when they are first summoned unless they are also a creature. Artifacts have integrity (health). Artifacts can be targeted by attacks. When an artifact is attacked, they get hit counters like creatures do. When an artifact has hit counters equal to or greater than its integrity, it gets destroyed and goes to the graveyard. Altho artifacts have health, they cannot block.

Enchantments can be enchanted onto another permanent using mana. Enchantments often state what kinds of valid permanents it can enchant. Enchantments cant be targeted like permanents can, but they can be targeted by effects that specifically mention they can target enchantments. Enchantments often have an exit clause, which is a condition that specifies how the enchantment can be broken.

Lands can be visited for free. However, you may only visit one land per turn. Lands grant bonus mana regen per turn and often bonus energy regen per turn. They also often have abilities. Lands grant mana cost reduction on certain types of permanents and creatures. Lands have a Defense (health). Lands can be targeted by attacks. When a Land “takes damage” it gets a hit counter. When it has hit counters greater than or equal to its Defense, it gets placed under the control of the player that gave it the last hit counter. Lands don’t get destroyed or die. Altho a land has health, it cannot block.

Character: you have one at the start of the game for free. It has a name, health amount (usually 20), mana regen (usually 1), energy regen (usually 1), and abilities, and a special condition that describes when they get bonus energy (and/or mana). A character grants cost reduction on certain creature types and/or permanents. A character can be targeted by attacks, spells, and abilities. When a character takes damage, they gain a hit counter. When it has hit counters greater than or equal to its health, it dies and its player loses the game. A character can block attacks, spells, and abilities targeted at other permanents. If a character blocks something that cannot validly target a character, then that character takes damage equal to the spell’s mana cost or the ability’s energy cost. A character can only block once per turn.

At the beginning of your turn, your character and all your permanents Heal 1 automatically. To heal, remove a hit counter from the permanent.

When you attack a land on your turn, it’s called a Siege. When you siege during a combat phase, you may only attack Lands. During your xsiege, creatures you cast don’t have summoning sickness, but must attack a land you’re sieging if they attack.

This game is designed to make it easy to design a custom deck with custom cards inspired by your favorite tv show / movie / videogame / etc.
