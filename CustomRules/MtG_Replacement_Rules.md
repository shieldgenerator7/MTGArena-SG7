MtG Replacement

This game is designed to make it easy to design a custom deck with custom cards inspired by your favorite tv show / movie / videogame / etc.

# Goal

Be the last player standing.

# Setup

Each player does these steps:

1.  Make a deck with at least 60 cards.
    1.  No more than 4 copies of any one card.
    2.  Must have at least 1 creature.
2.  Choose a creature card from your deck to be your character.
    1.  Place your character in your battle zone.
    2.  If its defense is less than 20, give it shield counters until its defense is 20.
3.  Shuffle your library.
4.  Draw 7 cards.
5.  Create your battle zone, mana pool, and energy pool.

# Gameplay

Each player takes their turn one at a time in order.

## Turn Phases

Each turn consists of these phases:

1.  Morning phase
    1.  Gain mana
    2.  Gain energy
    3.  Heal
    4.  Claim (optional)
    5.  Untap permanents
    6.  Draw a card
2.  Day phase
    1.  Play cards (optional)
    2.  Activate abilities (optional)
    3.  Attack (optional)
3.  Evening phase
    1.  Discard down to your hand size limit (7)

## Morning Phase

At the start of your turn, you do these things.

Gain mana. You always gain at least 1 mana. For each land you control, collect its passive mana. Add the mana to your mana pool. You have no max mana and mana carries over to the next round.

Gain energy. You don’t automatically gain energy. For each creature you control, you gain at least 1 energy. Add the energy to your energy pool.

Heal. Choose a permanent to heal 1. To heal, remove a damage counter from it.

Claim. Choose a land you control, and add 1 claim token of yours to that land or remove 1 claim token of another player from that land.

Untap all permanents you control: creatures, artifacts, and lands. Untap your character.

Draw a card from your library. If your library is empty, your character takes 1 damage.

## Day Phase

On your turn, during your Day phase, you can:

-   Play cards
-   Activate abilities
-   Attack
-   Siege

### Play cards

Each card has a mana cost to play. To cast it, remove the required mana from your mana pool.

When you play a land, you can spend any amount of extra mana. The land enters play in your battle zone with your claim tokens on it. The amount of claim tokens is equal to the amount of mana you spent to play it.

When you play a creature, it comes into play tapped. Tapped creatures can’t attack, block, or activate abilities, and their passive abilities aren’t active, and their triggered abilities don’t trigger.

### Activate abilities

Some permanents have activatable abilities that cost energy to activate. To activate it, remove the required energy from your energy pool.

### Attack

Each untapped creature can attack on your turn. To have a creature attack, tap it and declare which target it is attacking.

If the target is a creature, both creatures deal damage to each other at the same time. The amount of damage a creature deals is equal to their attack stat. Their attack stat is equal to their bonus attack plus their power (default bonus attack is 0). Damage is dealt to creatures in wound counters. When has an amount of wound counters equal to or greater than its defense, it dies and is sent to the graveyard.

If the target is an artifact, the attacking creature deals damage to the artifact. If the amount of damage is equal to or greater than the artifact’s defense, the artifact is destroyed and is sent to the graveyard.

If the target is multiple types, all applicable rules apply to it.

### Siege

You can siege a land to add/or remove claim counters on it. When you declare a siege, tap any amount of creatures you control to siege with. This counts as an attack on the land for the purposes of other players reacting.

When at least 1 creature goes unblocked and successfully sieges the land, calculate your total siege power. Your siege power is equal to the total power of the sieging creatures that got through. For each siege power, add 1 claim token of yours or remove 1 claim token of another player’s.

If you have the most claim tokens on a land, you control it.

You may only siege each land once per turn.

## On other players’ turns

There are things you can do when it’s not your turn. When another player plays a card, activates an ability, or attacks, you can react to it.

You can react by playing a card, activating an ability, or blocking.

### Play a card

You can play a card if that card has the Instant keyword. Its effect resolves before the effect of the action before it.

### Activate an ability

You can activate the ability of a creature you control. Spend the required amount of energy from your energy pool to activate it.

However, if the previous action was a creature ability activation, your creature must have power equal to or less than the other creature’s power in order to interrupt it *(“Smaller” creatures are faster)*.

### Block

You can block an attack. When an attack is declared, you can tap a creature you control to block the attack, causing the attack to instead target the blocking creature.

You can block a spell cast. When a spell targets one or more permanents, you can tap a creature you control to block the spell, causing it to change one of its targets to the blocking creature.

You can block an activated ability. When an activated ability targets one or more permanents, you can tap a creature you control to block the ability, causing it to change one of its targets to the blocking creature.

When you block, if the blocking creature is not a valid target for the spell or activated ability, then the spell or ability is wasted.

# Changes

-   Mana is always colorless.
-   You gain mana passively each turn.
-   There’s no max mana.
-   Mana carries over between turns.
-   Mana is used to cast spells and summon creatures.
-   Energy is used to activate creature abilities.
-   You passively gain energy each turn.
-   The card types are Creature, Land, Spell, Artifact.
-   You can tap a creature to attack.
-   You can attack creatures, artifacts, and lands.
-   You can tap a creature to block, changing the target of the attack, spell, or ability to the blocking creature.
-   Creatures don’t auto-heal at the end of each turn.
-   You no longer have a health pool.
-   You choose a creature to be your main character card.
-   Each creature has a power stat, which is their combined attack and defense.
-   Each creature may have a sword stat for additional attack, or a shield stat for additional defense.
-   Each creature has a mana cost to summon.
-   Each creature can have special abilities that trigger under certain conditions or cost energy to activate.
-   There is no max hand size. You still start with 7 cards, and draw 1 card from your library each turn.
-   When you go to draw a card while your library is empty, your character loses 1 health.
-   When your character drops to 0 health or less, you lose.
-   Each player has a hand, library, graveyard, exile pile, mana pool, energy pool, and battle zone.
-   Spell cards are like the old sorceries.
-   Spell cards with the “Instant” keyword can be cast in reaction to other cards.
-   Trample, Flying, Reach, Deathtouch, First Strike, Double Strike, Menace, Vigilance, Haste are all still a thing.
    -   Trample means excess damage is dealt to the original target.
    -   Lifelink is replaced by Lifesteal, which heals the combating creature.
    -   Vigilance means that the creature can untap after attacking or blocking.
-   Lands passively and/or actively give you mana or energy.
-   “Enchantment” is a keyword for spell cards that makes the effect permanent.
-   Artifacts cant attack or block, unless it is also a Creature.
-   Lands can be conquered. Instead of dealing damage, a creature can add and remove claim tokens. The player who has the most claim tokens on a land controls it.
-   Flash is now called “Instant”.
-   Split-second is not a keyword.
-   The library min size will still be 60.

# Card Types

Card types: Creature, Spell, Artifact, Land, Character.

## Creature

A creature is a card that stays on the battlefield and can attack and block, and have abilities.

Playing a creature is called “summoning”. When you summon a creature, it enters play tapped.

Tapped creatures cant attack, block, or activate their activatable abilities, and their passive abilities are inactive and their triggered abilities don’t trigger.

A Creature has a mana cost, power, bonus attack, bonus defense, and a name, tags, ability, and flavor text.

When they attack, they deal damage equal to their power plus their bonus attack. Their health is equal to their power plus bonus defense. When they have wound counters equal to their health, they die and get sent to the graveyard.

Creatures can target other permanents to attack, and creatures can be targeted by attacks. A creature can only attack once per turn, because it taps to do so.

If another permanent you control is being targeted for an attack, you may block that attack with a creature. The blocking creature takes the damage instead, and deals damage to the attacking creature.

If another permanent you control is being targeted by a spell or ability, you may block that spell or ability with a creature, and have that spell or ability target that creature instead. If a creature is not a valid target for the spell or ability it blocks, the spell or ability does nothing. A creature can block only once per turn, because it must tap to do so.

A creature dies if:

-   Its power is 0 or less
-   It has damage counters equal to or greater than its health

Character: you choose one of your creatures to be your character. It starts in the battle zone untapped. A character can be targeted by attacks, spells, and abilities. A character takes damage like a creature does. A character can be tapped to block attacks, spells, and abilities targeted at other permanents. When your character dies or leaves the battlefield, you lose the game.

## Spell

A Spell is a card that does an effect, and then goes away.

Playing a Spell is called “casting”. When you cast a spell, it takes its effect, and then it gets sent to the bottom of the library.

Some spells have the “Enchantment” keyword, which mean it stays attached to its target, and its effect lasts while it is in play. If it leaves play, its effect stops.

Enchantments are a Spell with the “Enchantment” keyword, meaning it attaches to its target and is permanent. Enchantments often state what kinds of valid permanents it can enchant. Enchantments can only be targeted by effects that specifically state they can target enchantments. Enchantments often have an exit clause, which is a condition that specifies how the enchantment can be broken. When an Enchantment is broken, it gets sent to the graveyard.

## Artifact

Artifacts get crafted using mana. They stay on the battlefield and have abilities. Artifacts come into play untapped, unless they are also a creature. Artifacts have integrity (health). Artifacts can be targeted by attacks. When an artifact is attacked, if the attack damage is equal to or greater than its integrity, it gets destroyed and goes to the graveyard. Artifacts cannot block unless it is also a creature.

Artifacts have these stats:

-   Mana cost
-   Durability

## Land

Lands can be discovered (played) by paying their mana cost. Lands enter play with an amount of claim tokens equal to the mana spent on it. Lands grant bonus mana regen per turn and often bonus energy regen per turn. They also often have abilities. Lands grant mana cost reduction on certain types of permanents and creatures. Lands can be targeted by creatures to add and remove claim tokens. Claim tokens are specific to each player. The player with the most claim tokens on a land controls it.

Lands have these stats:

-   Mana cost
-   Mana regen

# Keywords

These are keywords that modify cards to give them common abilities.

## Arrival

This keyword triggers when this card enters the battlefield.

## Banding [attack bonus]

When this creature attacks with another creature that shares a type, it gets +1 power until end of turn.

## Bond [tags]

It costs 1 mana less for you to summon a creature with the given tag(s). If there are multiple tags, the same creature gets this discount for each tag it has in the list. The discount can’t cause the mana cost to go below 0.

## Bane [tags]

It costs 1 mana more for you to summon a creature with the given tag(s). If there are multiple tags, the same creature gets this markup for each tag it has in the list.

## Break

This Spell Enchantment ends and is sent to the bottom of its owner’s library.

## Cleave

Before combat damage is assigned, this creature deals damage equal to its bonus attack to all creatures attacking or blocking it.

## Collectible [amount]

This card contributes to your total Collectible score.

At the start of your turn, if your total Collectible score is 10 or more, you win the game.

## Deathtouch

When this creature deals damage to another creature, that creature dies, regardless of how much health it has. However, if the creature is also an artifact, this keyword has no effect.

## Defender

This creature can’t tap to attack.

## Double Strike

This creature deals First Strike damage and regular damage.

## Enchantment

When this Spell enters play, it attaches to its target.

This Spell’s effect is active while it is attached to its target.

If the target leaves the battlefield, this spell returns to the bottom of its owner’s library.

## Equip [energy cost]

Pay the energy cost to attach this Artifact to a creature you control (“equipped creature”). While attached, this artifact can’t be targeted by attacks.

If equipped creature dies, this Artifact detaches and remains on the battlefield.

## First Strike

This creature deals damage first, possibly killing the other creature before it can do damage.

## Flying

This creature’s attacks and abilities can’t be blocked by other creatures, unless that other creature has Flying or Reach.

## Haste

This creature enters untapped.

## Heal X

Remove X damage counters from the target.

## Hexproof

This creature can’t be targeted by spells or abilities opponents control.

This creature can still become the target of spells or abilities that it blocks.

## Instant

This card can be played in reaction to another player’s action.

## Last Words

This keyword triggers when this creature dies.

## LIfesteal

When this creature deals damage, it heals the same amount. The healing is applied before the death check.

## Mentor

Give +1 power to target creature with less power than this creature.

## Reach

This creature can block attacks and abilities of creatures with Flying.

## Scan [criteria]

Look at the top cards of your library until you find a card that meets the criteria, reveal it, put it in your hand, then put the cards you looked at on the bottom of your library in a random order.

## Skittish

Whenever a creature with Attack greater than this creature’s Health arrives under an opponent’s control, exile this creature until end of turn.

## Trample

When this creature deals damage, if it kills the blocking creature, any excess damage counters carry over to the creature’s original target, converting as necessary depending on the type of the target.

## Vigilance

When this creature or attacks or blocks, and as a result, a creature you don’t control dies, this creature untaps.

Return target creature from your graveyard or from your exile to the battlefield. Then, place a +1/+1 counter on up to 1 Human Creature you control and up to 1 Horse Creature you control.
