Magic the Gathering Rule Changes

# Character

Each player chooses a creature to be their character, which starts the game on the battlefield untapped. It has an additional 20 toughness.

When your character dies or leaves the battlefield, you lose the game.

You the player no longer have a health pool.

# Ending the Game

When you would draw a card while your library is empty, your character takes 1 damage instead.

When you end your turn with more cards in hand than your max hand size, your character takes 1 damage. Discarding down to your hand size is optional.

# Mana

You passively gain 1 mana (colorless) at the start of your turn. You have no max mana and mana carries over between phases and turns.

Lands don’t tap for mana. Instead, they passively grant you +1 mana at the start of your turn. Lands grant colored mana in its color. You can choose which color a Land produces from among the colors it can produce.

This means you could play a deck with no Land cards and still be able to play cards.

# Creatures

Creatures enter play tapped. There is no more summoning sickness.

You can tap a creature to attack. You can attack creatures and artifacts, but not players.

Creatures deal damage to other creatures in damage counters. When a creature has damage counters equal to or greater than its toughness, it dies.

When a creature deals damage to an artifact, if the damage is equal to or greater than the artifact’s converted mana cost, the artifact is destroyed. Only artifacts that are not attached to another permanent are able to be targeted by an attack.

You can tap a creature to block an attack, spell, or ability. Change one of the targets to the blocking creature.

Creatures don’t auto-heal at the end of the turn. When a creature is healed, remove a damage counter from it.

# Combat

There is no combat phase, and thus no second main phase.

During your main phase, you can declare attackers at any time at sorcery speed. To declare an attack, tap any amount of creatures you control and send them to attack any valid target. Then opponents have a chance to declare blockers and tap creatures to block.

You can declare an attack any number of times during your main phase.

# Phases

At the start of your turn, after gaining mana, you can spend 1 mana to heal a creature for 1 health. You can do this any number of times before entering your draw step.

Combat phase and second main phase are removed.

Discarding down to your hand size is optional now. But you take damage if you don’t.

# Keywords

Deathtouch – When this creature deals damage to another non-character creature, that creature is destroyed.

Defender – This creature can’t attack, but can block without tapping.

Haste – This creature enters play untapped. If it entered and then gained Haste afterwards in the same turn, untap it.

Lifelink – This creature heals itself for the damage it deals.

Vigilance – When a permanent you control is targeted by an attack, spell, or ability you don’t control, untap this creature.

Trample – Excess damage is dealt to the original target.
